The challenge

Understanding swallowing difficulties

Dysphagia can affect people living with conditions such as stroke and Parkinson’s disease. Swallowing difficulties may require professional assessment and appropriate care.

SwallowGuard AI explores whether sensor signals could help researchers study swallowing-related patterns. It is not intended to diagnose a condition or replace clinical evaluation.

🧠

Neurological conditions

Swallowing difficulties can occur alongside stroke, Parkinson’s and other conditions.

ⓘ

Why monitoring matters

Careful assessment can help healthcare professionals understand an individual’s needs.

🤝

Research opportunity

Explore responsible, non-invasive ways to study swallowing-related signals.

Our innovation

A multi-sensor prototype concept

SwallowGuard AI is exploring the combination of accelerometer, microphone and surface electromyography (EMG) signals. These data streams may be studied with signal processing and AI methods to investigate swallowing-related patterns.

Current status: Early prototype development. Sensor suitability, signal quality, usability and performance still require systematic testing and expert guidance.
